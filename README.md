## About

**private vpn for privacy** is a no-nonsense VPN setup focused on keeping your traffic private and your connection stable. It’s built around a **privacy and security focus**: **AES-256 encryption**, a clear **No-Logs policy**, and a **Kill Switch** that cuts traffic if the tunnel drops.

- **AES-256 encryption** for traffic in transit

## FAQ

**1) Does this protect me on public Wi‑Fi?**  
Yes. The tunnel uses **AES-256 encryption**, which helps prevent sniffing on shared networks.

**2) What happens if the VPN connection drops?**  
Enable **Kill Switch**. It blocks traffic until the VPN reconnects, reducing leak risk.

**3) Do you keep logs?**  
The intent is a **No-Logs policy**: no storage of browsing activity.

**4) Will it slow down my connection?**  
Any VPN adds some overhead, but this is tuned for **high speed** and a **stable connection**, especially on nearby **Global Servers**.
